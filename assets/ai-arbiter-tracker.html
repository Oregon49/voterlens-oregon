How it works, in one paragraph

All the arguments are in — the briefs are filed, the argument is heard — and then the Court sits and works it out over months. The AI reads the same record and makes its ruling, and then we wait for the judges to come out with theirs. The fast cases work the same way on a shorter record: application, response, lower-court order, and the AI rules before the Court's order comes down. The ruling is a prediction, not a verdict: the Court's decision is the law, and the AI ruling is the check on it.
